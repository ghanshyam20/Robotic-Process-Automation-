# Process description: Student club expense reimbursement

*Fictional scenario for exercise purposes — no real organization, person, or financial data.*

The Metropolia Robotics Club has around 60 members and runs on a small annual budget. Members
who buy things for the club (soldering supplies, competition entry fees, printing costs, snacks
for events) get reimbursed by the club treasurer. Here's how it currently works.

When a member buys something for the club, they take a photo of the paper receipt with their
phone and email it to the treasurer, along with a short note saying what it was for and which
budget category it falls under (Equipment, Events, or Admin). The treasurer checks the email
inbox for these requests a few times a week — there's no fixed schedule, it depends on how busy
the treasurer is with coursework.

When the treasurer opens a request, the first thing they do is look at the receipt photo to make
sure it's actually readable — the total amount, the date, and the vendor name all need to be
visible. If the photo is too blurry or the receipt is cut off in the frame, the treasurer can't
proceed: they reply to the member asking for a clearer photo, and the request just sits
unprocessed until a usable one arrives. This happens fairly often — maybe one in eight requests —
usually because people photograph receipts in bad lighting.

Once the treasurer has a readable receipt, they check the amount against the club's
reimbursement policy: any single reimbursement up to €50 can be approved by the treasurer alone.
Anything over €50 needs a second signature — the treasurer forwards the request by email to the
club president, who reviews it and replies either approving or rejecting it. The treasurer waits
for that reply before doing anything further with requests in this bracket; there's no time
limit on how long the president takes to respond, which has occasionally meant a member waiting
two or three weeks for reimbursement.

Once a request is approved (either by the treasurer directly, or by the president for larger
amounts), the treasurer opens the club's expense ledger — a shared Excel file kept on the club's
Google Drive — and adds a new row: date, member name, amount, budget category, and a short
description copied from the original email. The treasurer also uploads a copy of the receipt
photo into a "Receipts" folder on the same Drive, named with the date and member's name, for the
annual audit the student union does every May.

With the ledger updated, the treasurer logs into the club's online banking portal and initiates a
transfer to the member's bank account for the approved amount. Members' IBANs are kept in a
separate spreadsheet the treasurer maintains from when people joined the club; occasionally a
member has since changed banks and the IBAN on file no longer works, in which case the transfer
is rejected by the bank and the treasurer has to email the member asking for their current IBAN
before trying again. Once the transfer goes through, the treasurer replies to the original email
thread letting the member know the payment has been sent.

On average the club processes 15–20 reimbursement requests per month, with noticeable spikes
around competition season (October–November) when equipment and travel costs go up.
