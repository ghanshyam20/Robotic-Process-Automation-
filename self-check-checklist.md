# Self-check checklist

Go through your completed `pdd-template.md` against this list. Check off each item — if you
can't check something off, that section needs another pass.

## Section 1 — Overview
- [ ] The goal is stated as an outcome ("member is reimbursed"), not a step ("treasurer opens
      email")
- [ ] Every actor mentioned later in the document (steps, decisions, exceptions) is listed here

## Section 2 — As-Is steps
- [ ] Every step has exactly one actor and one action — if you wrote "and" in an action, split it
      into two steps
- [ ] Steps are in the order they actually happen, including the approval wait and the ledger/
      receipt-filing step (easy to skip since it happens "in the background")
- [ ] You described what happens today, not a redesigned/automated version of it

## Section 3 — Business rules & decision points
- [ ] The €50 threshold rule is captured, with both paths (treasurer-only vs. president
      approval) described
- [ ] Each path says what happens next, not just "approved" / "rejected"

## Section 4 — Exceptions
- [ ] At least one **business exception** is documented (a valid-but-different path — the
      approval-threshold branch counts, but check whether it belongs here or in Section 3, or
      both)
- [ ] At least one **application exception** is documented, with the fallback the treasurer
      actually uses today (not an ideal fallback — the real one, even if it's just "email the
      member and wait")
- [ ] You checked whether business and application exceptions got mixed up — a blurry receipt is
      an application exception (something's broken: the input is unusable); the €50 threshold is
      a business exception (a valid rule, not a failure)

## Section 5 — Scope
- [ ] At least one out-of-scope item names *why* it's out of scope (needs judgment, needs a
      human signature, etc.) rather than just listing it

## Section 6 — Volumes, frequency & systems
- [ ] Volume and frequency are both filled in with numbers/ranges from the scenario, not just
      "sometimes" or "regularly"
- [ ] Every system mentioned in the narrative is listed (there are more than two — count email,
      the receipt photos themselves, the shared Drive/Excel ledger, the Drive receipts folder,
      online banking, and the separate IBAN spreadsheet)

## If you finished early
- [ ] You found and documented the second exception case (bank transfer rejected due to an
      out-of-date IBAN) in addition to the blurry-receipt one covered in the lecture's worked
      example
